About the label

Jordan Kirkwood Music Group (JKMG) is a next‑generation record label bridging independent artistry and global discovery. We focus on genre-expansive music — from alternative electronic to modern indie, experimental pop, and beyond.

With a curator’s ear and an artist-first philosophy, JKMG provides distribution, strategic playlisting, and creative development. Our mission is to elevate voices that challenge conventions and resonate deeply with listeners worldwide.

Since our launch, we have partnered with Spotify for Artists, digital stores, and sync licensing to ensure our roster reaches the right audiences. The label is proud to be fully owned and operated by Jordan Kirkwood — a passionate A&R, producer, and music executive.
